Before you apply

What we look for, and answers to what people ask most.

Application criteria

For apartments. Every adult on the lease applies separately, and most decisions come back within two business days.

  • IncomeHousehold income of 2.5 times the monthly rent
  • Credit and backgroundChecked for every adult 18 and over
  • Rental historyTwo years of positive references, or a co-signer
  • Application fee$45 per adult, non-refundable
  • Holding deposit$300, credited to your first month
  • PetsUp to two. $300 pet deposit and $35 a month per pet
  • Retail tenantsBusiness plan and two years of financials

Questions

Still unsure? Call the leasing office at (555) 010-2400.

How do I reserve a storage unit?

Pick a unit on the storage plan, add it to your cart and choose a move-in date. Your gate code and rental agreement appear in your ADR account. Nothing is charged until move-in day.

Can I tour an apartment before applying?

Yes. Book a tour from any listing and we'll confirm a time by text the same day. Self-guided tours are available at The Foundry and Riverside Lofts.

How fast are repairs handled?

You get a reply within 24 hours and can follow each step in the portal. Emergencies such as no heat, active leaks or lockouts are answered at any hour.

Can I switch to a bigger or smaller storage unit?

Yes. Move to any open unit at the same facility with no new admin fee. Request the switch from your account.

What do retail lease rates include?

Each listing says whether it is NNN, where you also pay a share of taxes, insurance and common-area costs, or modified gross, where some of those are included. The estimated monthly total is on every listing.

How do I pay rent?

Pay online by bank transfer or card, set up autopay, or drop a check at the leasing office. Storage and retail charges are paid from the same account.
